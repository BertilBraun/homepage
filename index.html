LLM systems · Technical report

LLM-Light — Artifact-Addressed Training

Related LLM experiments share expensive preparation and pretraining, but you still want to describe each one as a single self-contained configuration. LLM-Light keeps that abstraction: every stage artifact is keyed by its declared computation, so compatible runs converge on one completed artifact instead of recomputing it — and the executor just runs whatever is left.

Computer vision · Live demo

GybeLock — Windsurfing Video Intelligence

Shore-shot windsurfing footage is useless for reviewing your own riding — you are forty pixels tall and the camera shakes. GybeLock trains YOLO detection and pose models on exactly that footage, tracks every rider through the clip, and solves for a stabilisation transform, so clicking one sailor gives a steady view that follows them. Shipped end to end: training, serving, and the player itself.

Graph RL · Technical report

Graph-Based Traffic Signal Control

A shared graph network scores individual traffic movements; each junction then assembles its own variable-sized set of legal phases from those scores through a deterministic incidence matrix. One policy instance ran across networks from 41 to 84 controlled junctions, with 2 to 12 phases each.
